This sketch explores different compositions using three simple elements: a circle, triangle, and line. With each click, their size, rotation, color, and arrangement randomly change. I also added a black stroke around the triangle to maintain contrast within the composition as the other colors change. In my original sketches, I experimented with more variations in the positions of the circle and triangle while still keeping them opposite from each other to minimize overlap. In the coded version, their positions are more structured, switching between four opposite corners, while the random sizes, colors, and rotations create more unexpected combinations.
